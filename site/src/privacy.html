Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

Clacket runs offline. It reads your keyboard and mouse only to play a sound on your own computer, and it never records, keeps or sends anything you type. No account, no analytics, no telemetry. The only time we hold any data about you is when you buy Pro, and then only what we need to give you a license and let you recover it. That's it.

What the app collects: nothing

Clacket works offline and never connects to the internet. When you type or move the mouse, it reads those events only to play the matching sound, and then it's done. It doesn't record what you type, keep it, or send it anywhere. The licence check also happens on your own computer, offline, using an Ed25519 signature.

What we collect when you buy Pro

To issue your license and let you recover it later, we store a few things in our database (Cloudflare D1):

  • Your email and name, as you gave them to Stripe.
  • Your license key and its id.
  • The Stripe checkout and payment reference.
  • A flag for whether the key has been refunded.
  • The related timestamps.

We keep this to issue your key, confirm the payment went through, and find your key for you if you lose it. We don't send marketing, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Stripe handles the payment

Payment runs through Stripe. We never see or store your card details, those stay with Stripe. Your purchase is also covered by Stripe's own privacy policy.

We don't email you

We don't send you any email. Your key appears on the "thanks" page right after you pay, and you can download it as a clacket.license file. "Lost your key?" is a help page, not an email from us. (Stripe may send you its own payment receipt, that one comes from Stripe.)

The website and Cloudflare

The website at clacket.th33raphat.dev is a static site hosted on Cloudflare. When someone visits, Cloudflare may keep standard request logs and security data as our processor. The in-browser demo loads sound and image files from our server, but we don't track visitors.

No tracking cookies

This site doesn't use cookies to track you.

Refund and revocation

When you ask for a refund, we set your license to revoked in our database. After that, the key can't unlock Pro on a new computer, and a future version of the app stops honouring it.

How long we keep your data

We keep the data tied to a license while the license is valid, and for as long as we need it for support and accounting.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, just email us. (If you have us delete your license data, we may no longer be able to recover your key for you later.)

Not for children

Clacket isn't aimed at children, and we don't set out to collect children's data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update the "last updated" date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your data, or want to see, correct or delete it? Email th33raphat@gmail.com.
