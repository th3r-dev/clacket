Free and Pro

Clacket is free to use with four switches and the basics. Clacket Pro is a one-time purchase of $5 that unlocks the rest: all 13 switches, a sound for any key, your own sound files, presets, and mouse, trackpad and plug-in sounds. Pro is for your personal use. Please don't share or resell your key.

One active device per license, transferable

A Pro license runs on one device at a time. When you activate, Clacket sends your license key and a salted device fingerprint to our server, which binds the key to that device (see the Privacy Policy for exactly what's sent). To move Pro to another computer, deactivate it on the first one, which frees the license, then activate on the new one. Both steps need an internet connection. If a device is lost, broken, or wiped and you can't deactivate it, contact us and we'll free the seat for you.

Refunds

If Pro isn't for you, ask for a refund by replying to your Stripe receipt. A refund switches the license off: it's revoked and its device seat is freed, so Pro stops working on that machine.
