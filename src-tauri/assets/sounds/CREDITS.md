# Sound credits

## Keyboard switches
From [kbsim](https://github.com/tplai/kbsim) by Thomas Lai, commit `ba103f3`, under the MIT license (see `LICENSE-kbsim.md`).
Switches: Alpaca, Gateron Black Ink, Blue Alps, Box Navy, Buckling Spring, NK Cream, Holy Panda, Cherry MX Black, MX Blue, MX Brown, Gateron Red Ink, Topre, Turquoise Tealios.

## Mouse, gesture and plug sounds (`fx/`)
From Kenney's [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) and [UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio), CC0 (public domain).
`fx/move.mp3` was generated with ffmpeg (brown noise, low-pass).

Run `scripts/fetch-sounds.sh` to rebuild this folder.
